Privacy Policy

Forge — workout & nutrition tracker

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Forge is built to keep your data on your device. We do not run accounts, servers, ads, analytics, or third-party tracking of any kind.

What Forge stores, and where

Everything you log — workouts, routines, foods and meals, body weight and measurements, water, steps, medications, goals and settings — is saved only in local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it.

Health & step data

If you connect Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android), Forge reads your daily step count with your permission, purely to display it inside the app. This data is processed on your device and stored locally alongside your other entries. It is not transmitted anywhere. You can revoke the permission at any time in your system Health settings, and Forge only ever requests read access to steps.

Network use

  • Food search (optional): when you search foods under the “Online” tab, the words you type are sent to the Open Food Facts public API to fetch results. No identity or personal data is attached to that request.
  • Fonts: the web version loads fonts from Google Fonts. No account data is involved.

Nothing else is sent over the network. The app works fully offline.

Backups you create

You can export your data as a JSON or CSV file, and import it back. These files are created and stored by you, wherever you choose. Forge does not upload or receive them.

Data sharing & deletion

We never sell or share your data — we never even receive it. To delete everything, use Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

Children

Forge is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
